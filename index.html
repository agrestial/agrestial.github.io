Services

Complete engineering design support from concept design to validated product.

Agrestial Engineering delivers a full product development package built around practical, iterative engineering design. We help clients shape concepts, produce accurate CAD models and drawings, assess performance with FEA and CFD, and prepare components for CNC machining and 3D printing. Our manufacturing services enable rapid prototyping and fast iteration, allowing designs to be tested, refined, and proven with real hardware early in the development process.

Concept Design

Early-stage engineering focused on building a clear understanding of the problem before any design work begins. This includes structured requirements capture, analysis of the problem space, and feasibility-led concept generation that establishes credible routes for downstream engineering.

  • Understanding the problem
  • Requirements capture
  • Concept generation

CAD Design & Documentation

Detailed mechanical design built on accurate, parametric CAD modelling and clear technical documentation. Work focuses on producing robust part and assembly models, engineering drawings that capture the design intent, and revision‑managed outputs that support manufacture, review, and project coordination.

  • 3D part and assembly modelling
  • Engineering drawings that capture the design intent
  • Revision‑controlled technical documentation

Simulation: FEA & CFD

Engineering simulation support to evaluate structural behaviour, thermal effects, and fluid performance, helping guide design decisions, reduce risk, and strengthen confidence before manufacture or testing.

  • Finite Element Analysis (FEA)
  • Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
  • Performance-led design improvement

Manufacturing Support

Our manufacturing services in CNC machining and 3D printing bring your part to life with rapid, reliable delivery. Using established manufacturing partners, we handle the full route from prepared CAD data to finished components — managing prototypes, iterations, and production parts so you receive hardware that matches the design intent.

  • CNC machining and 3D printing delivered through trusted partners
  • Rapid prototyping with coordinated build management
  • End‑to‑end support from CAD data to finished parts

Physical Testing & Validation

Practical, real‑world testing to verify whether a design performs as intended. We support structural testing, prototype evaluation, and evidence‑based validation activities that confirm performance, highlight issues, and guide refinement before manufacture or release.

  • Structural testing of physical components
  • Prototype evaluation and comparison against expected behaviour
  • Evidence‑based input for design refinement and decision‑making

Mechanical Design Contracting

Flexible contracting support that provides experienced mechanical design capability exactly where it’s needed. We deliver self‑contained work packages or integrate into existing engineering teams, supplying structured design input, CAD development, simulation, documentation, and manufacturing support to keep projects moving.

  • Short‑term or ongoing mechanical design support
  • Independent delivery of defined engineering work packages
  • CAD, analysis, documentation, and manufacturing coordination as required

About

The Agrestial Approach

By definition, Agrestial describes something that grows wild and naturally - unconfined, adaptable and shaped by its environment. We chose the name because it reflects how we approach engineering.


The best solutions develop through exploration, refinement and a clear understanding of the environment in which they need to perform. Our role is to understand those influences, bring structure to the process and help great ideas develop into robust, practical solutions.

Agrestial represents an engineering approach that is curious, adaptable and grounded in the real world—with the freedom to explore, and the discipline to deliver.
